# Call 7 Bug Report

Recording: `recordings/call7.mp3`
Scenario: Cancel an appointment scheduled for tomorrow and ask for a cancellation confirmation number.

## Bug: Greeting is interrupted

Severity: Medium

What happened: The opening greeting was interrupted.

Why it matters: The call starts less naturally, and the receptionist may still be giving the opening prompt.

Fix: Wait for the greeting to finish before starting the cancellation request.

## Bug: Ending cuts off

Severity: Low

What happened: The cancellation reason was good, and the confirmation-number follow-up was good. The ending still cut off around the final thank-you.

Why it matters: The call should end cleanly so it is obvious the task is done.

Fix: Let the final thank-you/goodbye finish before ending the call.
