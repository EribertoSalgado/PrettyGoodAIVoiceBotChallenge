# Call 12 Bug Report

Recording: `recordings/call12.mp3`
Scenario: Reschedule an existing appointment to next week in the morning.

## Bug: Greeting is slow

Severity: Low

What happened: The greeting still felt slow.

Why it matters: Slow starts can make the caller speak too early.

Fix: Reduce the greeting delay while still waiting for the receptionist to finish.

## Bug: Receptionist interrupts clarification

Severity: Medium

What happened: The conversation was good overall, but the receptionist interrupted when the patient was clarifying the confusion.

Why it matters: Clarification is important during rescheduling. Cutting it off can lead to the wrong appointment or preference.

Fix: Let the patient finish the clarification before asking the next question.
