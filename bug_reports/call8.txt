# Call 8 Bug Report

Recording: `recordings/call8.mp3`
Scenario: Ask about office hours and weekend availability, then schedule during the week.

## Bug: Intro takes too long

Severity: Low

What happened: The intro had a long wait, and the receptionist asked twice if this was John.

Why it matters: It makes the beginning feel less smooth and can make the caller unsure when to start.

Fix: Keep the identity check, but avoid repeating it unless the answer was unclear.

## Bug: Transfer path feels a little circular

Severity: Medium

What happened: The receptionist handled the transfer pretty well overall, but kept trying to support the patient after the patient had already asked for support. Since the receptionist did not have the information the patient needed, that extra step felt unnecessary.

Why it matters: If the patient asks for a person because the receptionist cannot answer, the call should move forward instead of looping.

Fix: Transfer once the patient asks for support in that situation.
