# Call 5 Bug Report

Recording: `recordings/call5.mp3`
Scenario: Reschedule an orthopedic appointment for lower back pain to a morning time.

## Bug: Patient caller talks too early

Severity: Medium

What happened: The caller greeted too fast while the receptionist was still talking. The call also had multiple interruptions.

Why it matters: It makes the conversation sound rushed, and appointment details can get missed.

Fix: Wait until the receptionist fully finishes before answering.

## Bug: Reschedule reason could be clearer

Severity: Low

What happened: The patient should give a clearer reason for rescheduling. Also, when something needs to be repeated, the caller should use more natural phrases than just "sorry."

Why it matters: A clear reason helps the receptionist move the call forward without extra back-and-forth.

Fix: Give a short reason when asked, like needing a morning appointment, and use lines like "Could you repeat that?" or "I did not catch the time."
