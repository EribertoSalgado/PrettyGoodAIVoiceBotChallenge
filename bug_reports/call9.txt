# Call 9 Bug Report

Recording: `recordings/call9.mp3`
Scenario: Request a medication refill.

## Bug: Greeting is slow and introduction gets cut off

Severity: Medium

What happened: The greeting response was slow. The receptionist also interrupted while the patient was introducing themself. This seemed more likely when the patient said more than just their name.

Why it matters: The first turn is where the patient gives identity and reason for calling. Cutting it off creates extra cleanup later.

Fix: Let the patient finish the first sentence before asking the next question.

## Bug: Refill scenario needs full pharmacy details

Severity: Low

What happened: The phone number correction and timeout response were good. The patient caller still needs a defined pharmacy and address ready for the refill test.

Why it matters: A real refill call usually needs pharmacy details.

Fix: Preload the refill scenario with pharmacy name, address, and phone number.
